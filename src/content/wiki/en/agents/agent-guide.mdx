## Which Agent should you try first?

Start with Nik to learn movement and resource decisions, then try an unlocked Agent whose ability supports your preferred setup. Elise is useful for companion support, James for weapon switching, and Lúcia for Curios. Pick a kit you can use consistently before taking on abilities that require risky positioning or spending health.

| What you want to practise | Agent to consider | Useful feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic exploration and combat | Nik | Available immediately, so you can focus on the run |
| Keeping companions active | Elise | Kiddy helps nearby dazed Hatchmon recover |
| Using complementary weapons | James | A second weapon slot lets you cover different attack situations |
| Managing active tools | Lúcia | An additional Curio slot gives you another equipped option |

This is a starting point for choosing a playstyle. Test how the ability fits your movement and the drops you find. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) covers the controls and resource habits that help with any Agent.

<Callout type="tip" title="Read the ability before starting">
Check the Agent profile and your bindings in the Bar. The Passive input used by several Agents is separate from the input that activates a Curio.
</Callout>

## What does each Agent's ability do?

Choose an Agent by the action their signature ability asks you to manage. Some bonuses work automatically, while others use the Passive input to switch equipment or interact with resources. Match that action to your comfort level: a powerful-looking kit is less useful if it distracts you from dodging or leaves you short of supplies.

| Agent | Signature ability | What to account for during a run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nik](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/nik/) | Legacy Watch produces coins when he is hurt | Treat the coins as a bonus after a hit; keep protecting your health |
| [Elise](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/elise/) | Plush Gloves supplies Kiddy, who awakens nearby dazed Hatchmon | Supports a squad that needs help staying active |
| [Xuan](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/xuan/) | Grazing bullets charges Clarity Gourd; Passive uses a full charge to clear nearby bullets and release talismans and a purple wisp | Grazing means approaching a bullet without being hit; practise the distance carefully |
| [Saya](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/saya/) | Rabbit & Bear allows switching to her katana, whose level follows the primary weapon | Learn the range of both attacks; this Agent requires DLC access |
| [James](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/james/) | Wedding Rings adds a weapon slot and increases Soul Shards gained from weapon extraction | Use Passive to switch; compare a spare before extracting it |
| [Lúcia](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/lucia/) | Moon Crystal adds a Curio slot and restores 1 crystal after battle | Switch tools with Passive and budget for each activation |
| [R-7](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/r-7/) | Abnormal Chip uses shields for health, launches tech missiles, and gains weapon damage from shield pickups | Hearts can become shields at Death's Door; ordinary healing needs different planning |
| [Mira](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/mira/) | Taking damage creates clown bombs and draws a buff card; the card bonuses reset each floor | Account for the health spent and the floor reset |
| [Cassie](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/cassie/) | Carousel Music Box attempts to unlock or steal goods; Luck affects success, and failure has a penalty | Read the result before making another attempt |
| [Tino](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/tino/) | Broken Trinket starts with a random Wonder Jar and two Wonder slots; Passive copies the held jar and triggers damage | Budget health before copying a jar |

For R-7, each shield pickup adds **0.5% weapon damage**, up to **100%** from this effect. His [ability entry](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/agents/r-7/) describes the shield-based scaling.

## How do you unlock the other Agents?

Complete the Agent's task, return to the Bar, and claim the finished reward on the Task screen. Read the condition carefully before planning a run. Collecting a total over several attempts, holding a quantity at once, and using a resource during one attempt are different requirements and need different approaches.

| Agent | Task | Requirement | Progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nik | Available immediately | Begin playing | No unlock task |
| Elise | Hatching Rookie | Successfully hatch eggs 8 times | Cumulative |
| Xuan | Shadow Syndicate | Hold 10 purple wisps simultaneously | One run |
| Saya | Emerging Spark | Reach Floor 5 | Reach the floor; DLC access required |
| James | Weapon Virtuoso | Use 30 different weapons | Cumulative |
| Lúcia | Magic Appreciation | Pick up 30 different Curios | Cumulative |
| R-7 | Iron Bastion | Hold 7 shields simultaneously | One run |
| Mira | Explosion Sparks | Use 30 bombs | One run |
| Cassie | Treasure Hunter | Open 100 locked chests | Cumulative |
| Tino | Magic Jar | Use 30 qualifying Wonder Jars | Cumulative |

The linked Agent names in the ability table lead to their corresponding unlock entries. Use the Task screen to follow your current progress and claim rewards after an attempt.

For a focused next step, the [Elise guide](/agents/elise-guide/) covers hatching progress and Kiddy's recovery, while the [R-7 guide](/agents/r-7-guide/) separates the seven-shield unlock from pickup-based damage scaling.

<Accordion title="What counts as progress for these tasks?">
<p>For James and Lúcia, the requirement concerns different weapons or Curios, so repeatedly using one familiar item is not the same as discovering new ones. For R-7 and Xuan, the required amount must be present simultaneously. Mira's condition tracks bombs used during the attempt.</p>
<p>For Tino, some jars that generate loot or rooms do not advance Magic Jar. Use the Task counter to check progress rather than assuming every jar action counts. The <a href="https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/guides/agents-unlocks/">unlock details</a> list those exceptions.</p>
</Accordion>

## How should you build around your chosen Agent?

Support the ability your Agent already has, then use new pickups to cover a practical weakness. Companion recovery pairs naturally with an active Hatchmon squad, while extra equipment slots reward complementary tools. Keep enough resources to use those tools, and test unfamiliar combinations before relying on them in a boss fight.

- **Elise:** Look for useful companions and effects that support their attacks. Kiddy helps recovery, while your own movement still determines whether you avoid damage.
- **James:** Choose weapons that solve different problems, such as reach and close-range pressure. A second slot is useful when you understand when to switch.
- **Lúcia:** Pair Curios that serve different needs, then budget their costs. Crystal recovery after battle helps planning but does not remove activation costs.
- **Xuan:** Practise grazing and using a charged gourd without abandoning your escape route.
- **R-7:** Look for shield support and understand how his health differs before spending on healing.

The [Hatchmon guide](/hatchmon/hatchmon-guide/) explains companion recovery and fusion options for a pet-focused setup.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the price of an ability">
Nik and Mira benefit after damage, and Tino's copying ability triggers damage. Decide whether the benefit is worth the health spent before deliberately activating a risky effect.
</Callout>

## What should you check before a boss fight or co-op run?

Check the equipment you are actually carrying, practise any switching input, and plan how your ability will help during the fight. In co-op, discuss who needs a pickup before taking it. Complementary tools give you more options, but every player still needs to manage movement, health, and their own activation costs.

<Accordion title="How can you prepare your equipment slots?">
<p>With James, check the reach and attack behaviour of both weapons before entering. With Lúcia, select the Curio you intend to activate and make sure you can pay its cost. With Tino, check the held jar and your health before copying it.</p>
<p>Keep the first attempt simple. Learn one ability interaction at a time, then expand the build when you can use it while dodging comfortably.</p>
</Accordion>

Use the [boss guide](/bosses/boss-guide/) to prepare for the fight itself. Your character choice should support that preparation rather than replace it.
