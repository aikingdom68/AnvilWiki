## How do you unlock and reach Dark Aphrodite?

Defeat regular Aphrodite to unlock the Dark encounter, then take an available Hermes route with at least ten Hatchmon present when Hermes is defeated. Treat the unlock and the run condition as separate tasks. Preparing a large squad before the regular victory does not replace the prerequisite, and the victory alone does not supply the companions.

The [official Wonder Flow announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2235200/announcements/detail/716781767925695624) specifies the regular Aphrodite victory and the **at least 10 Hatchmon** condition for defeating Hermes.

| Gate | Requirement | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encounter unlock | Defeat regular Aphrodite | Confirm the prerequisite victory |
| Route access | Hermes available at the safehouse | Choose the intended portal |
| Companion condition | At least 10 Hatchmon when Hermes is defeated | Inspect the squad before the fight |

This page covers access and preparation. Use the [general boss guide](/bosses/boss-guide/) for the movement and equipment decisions that help you complete the preceding encounters.

## What is the order of the route decisions?

Complete the regular Aphrodite prerequisite before planning a dedicated Dark route attempt. During that later run, develop the companion squad through the dungeon, choose Hermes at the safehouse, and verify the count before entering. The decisive requirement belongs to the Hermes victory, so the plan should preserve both the squad goal and your ability to finish that fight.

1. Defeat regular Aphrodite to open the Dark encounter.
2. Begin an attempt with a character you can use comfortably.
3. Build the companion squad while exploring the dungeon.
4. At the safehouse after Floor 5, select Hermes if available.
5. Recheck the Hatchmon count and combat supplies before the Hermes fight.
6. Defeat Hermes while meeting the companion requirement.
7. Continue into the Dark Aphrodite branch.

The [current route map](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/guides/boss-route/) places the safehouse between the dungeon and the chosen later route. An earlier boss unlock does not force every run to offer the same encounters.

<Callout type="tip" title="Plan before the portal">
Develop the squad during exploration. Choosing the Hermes portal with too few companions and no affordable hatching tool leaves less room to fix the route condition.
</Callout>

## How can you develop the required Hatchmon squad?

Collect reachable eggs and use available hatching effects that fit the actions and resources of your run. Keep the companion count visible in your plan rather than judging progress from egg inventory alone. Choose useful hatching tools when they appear, then preserve enough crystals or other resources to keep those tools affordable alongside combat preparation.

| Tool or direction | Contribution to the plan | Cost or condition to remember |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lightbulb](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/items/#lightbulb) | Hatches an egg immediately | Requires an egg and 2 crystals |
| [Replica Circlet](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/items/#replica-circlet) | Adds progress from crystal-consuming Curio use | Its trigger has a 3-second cooldown |
| [Godfather Disc](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/items/#godfather-disc) | Enemy kills can add hatching progress | Maintain a weapon that clears rooms reliably |
| Agapeism | Egg and hatch actions support its Faith meter | Choose it when available and useful for your plan |

The [Hatchmon guide](/hatchmon/hatchmon-guide/) covers hatching effects and fusion recipes, while the [Faith guide](/guides/faith-and-temples/) explains the Agapeism Temple direction.

<Callout type="warn" title="Inspect the Relic before spending">
Eternal Stone prevents hatching. Read its trade-off before committing more resources to growing the squad for this route.
</Callout>

## Why should you check the count after changing the squad?

Inspect the companion count again after a fusion, pickup, or other lineup change instead of relying on a number remembered from an earlier floor. A fusion can improve recovery or movement, but the route still asks for the required squad at the Hermes victory. Decide whether the change helps that attempt before committing to the fight.

| Checkpoint | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| After developing the squad | You are tracking companions rather than only eggs |
| After a lineup change | The current count still supports the route goal |
| Before selecting Hermes | You have a workable plan to meet the condition |
| Before the Hermes encounter | The squad and your combat supplies are ready |

<Accordion title="Should you prioritise companion count or fusion utility?">
<p>For this route, the minimum companion condition remains a requirement. Recovery and movement are useful only alongside a squad that still meets the plan.</p>
<p>Inspect the result after a change. Keep a helpful fusion when it improves the attempt and leaves the condition achievable; avoid assuming every companion combination preserves the earlier count.</p>
</Accordion>

## Which Agent and weapon can support the attempt?

Choose a kit you can operate comfortably while developing and recovering the squad. Elise provides Kiddy for awakening support, and Noxmare lets companion hits contribute to poison marks if you find it. Neither choice is required by the route. A familiar weapon and affordable Curio can be more useful than forcing a setup around drops that never appeared.

| Option | Why it may help | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elise | Kiddy supports dazed companions | Preserve the Agent's own health and position |
| Chirpie | Adds combat awakening support | Check the squad after its fusion |
| Noxmare | Companion hits add poison marks | Read the actual weapon affix and keep attacking |
| A familiar weapon | Reliable damage while you move | The route does not demand a particular weapon |

Use the [Elise guide](/agents/elise-guide/) for her recovery rhythm and the [Noxmare build](/hatchmon/noxmare-poison-build/) if that weapon fits the drops available to you.

<Accordion title="What should you do if the ideal weapon does not appear?">
<p>Continue with an attack you can use safely. The route condition is the unlock and the Hatchmon requirement, not possession of Noxmare or any other named weapon.</p>
<p>Choose upgrades that solve the actual problem in your attempt, such as awkward reach, unaffordable support activation, or insufficient protection.</p>
</Accordion>

## What should you check before Hermes falls?

Prepare the squad and combat budget before entering, then prioritise completing the encounter while keeping the route requirement in view. Check health, weapon reach, movement, and the resources for support activations. A large squad can contribute damage, but incoming attacks still require your attention; crossing danger to force one more interaction can lose the attempt entirely.

| Final preparation | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Prerequisite | Regular Aphrodite has been defeated |
| Squad | At least 10 Hatchmon available for the Hermes victory condition |
| Health or shields | Collect reachable protection before entering |
| Weapon | Know the attack range and how to fire while moving |
| Curio | Keep enough resources for the effect you intend to activate |
| Recovery | Know whether you will use safe manual awakening or companion support |

After a failed attempt, identify the missing gate or the survival problem before changing the whole build. The [current route map](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/guides/boss-route/) distinguishes the branches: **Dark Aphrodite ends the Dark route**, while regular Aphrodite can lead to Theia once unlocked.
