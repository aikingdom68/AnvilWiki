## How do you unlock and choose a Faith?

Unlock Path of Faith on the Evolution Tree, then use a Faith Pillar in the starting room of a dungeon floor to choose an available Faith. Consumerism is the initial unlock. Other Faiths require their own progression, so a missing option may need an unlock before it can appear among the pillar's choices.

The [Faith unlock entries](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/faiths/) distinguish the system unlock from individual options:

| Faith | Unlock to check | Run direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumerism | Path of Faith | Spending coins |
| Agapeism | Agapeism node | Gaining and hatching eggs |
| Artisanism | Craftsmanship node | Absorbing Soul Shards |
| Fortunism | Fortunism node | Gambling machines |
| Thesaurism | Treasure Dream node | Keys and unlocking |
| Umbrism | Tenebrism node | Wisps |
| Dualism | Duotheism node | Crystal and Spike Objects |
| Psychedelism | Defeat Eris and claim Sudden Clarity | Collecting pickups |

Choose from the options actually offered rather than delaying every decision for a Faith you have not unlocked. The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) covers the resource habits that help you make that choice.

## Which Faith fits the resources you already have?

Select a Faith whose progress comes from useful actions you expect to perform during the floor. A shopping plan fits Consumerism, while an egg-focused run fits Agapeism and weapon progression fits Artisanism. Avoid spending your last resource solely to move the meter when that purchase or interaction leaves the build unable to function.

| Current plan | Faith to consider | Why it matches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy healing or useful shop items | Consumerism | Coin spending builds progress |
| Develop an active companion squad | Agapeism | Eggs and hatches build progress |
| Extract spares and upgrade a weapon | Artisanism | Soul Shards support its meter |
| Open worthwhile locked rewards | Thesaurism | Key-related actions build progress |
| Collect and use wisps | Umbrism | Wisp acquisition supports it |
| Use gambling machines with spare coins | Fortunism | Gambling interactions contribute |
| Collect pickups while exploring | Psychedelism | Pickup acquisition supports it |
| Manage Crystal and Spike interactions | Dualism | Different interactions support Wisdom or Violence |

These are matching suggestions rather than a universal strength ranking. A Faith is more useful when its preferred actions also improve the run.

For companion choices, follow the [Hatchmon guide](/hatchmon/hatchmon-guide/). For a shard-focused plan, use the [weapon upgrade guide](/guides/weapon-upgrades/) to compare extraction costs and rewards.

## How do you build the meter without wasting resources?

Continue clearing rooms and perform the actions your selected Faith rewards when they serve a useful purpose. Watch the meter as you collect or spend resources, then plan a return to the pillar before leaving the floor. A Temple opportunity is valuable only if you can reach it and still afford the interactions you want inside.

For Agapeism, the current [Faith data](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/faiths/#agapeism) gives these contributions:

| Action | Faith progress | Planning implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear a room | +5 | Normal exploration contributes |
| Gain an egg | +10 | Egg pickups support the meter before hatching |
| Hatch an egg | +15 | Successful hatches provide another contribution |

Getting an egg and hatching it are distinct actions. An egg-focused Faith supports a companion plan, but choosing it does not replace the hatching effects and tools described in the [Elise guide](/agents/elise-guide/).

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep your next useful purchase possible">
Before spending just to finish a meter, check what remains for healing, Curio activation, or a Temple purchase. Progress that empties the resources you need can leave the rest of the floor harder to manage.
</Callout>

## When should you return to the Temple portal?

Return to the Faith Pillar when the meter reaches its full value of 100, then use the revealed portal before descending. Faith progress resets when you leave the floor, so continuing past the exit can waste that opportunity. Check the Temple's options, spend deliberately, and use its return stone to resume the dungeon.

<StatBar label="Faith meter: Temple-ready state" value={100} note="100 Faith / full meter" />

The [Temple rules](https://capydb.com/neon-abyss-2/guides/faith-temples/) give a practical visit sequence:

1. Notice that the meter is full.
2. Return to the pillar using a safe route.
3. Enter the Temple portal.
4. Inspect the available machines, items, and their costs.
5. Use the return stone when you are done.
6. Finish the floor with the supplies you kept available.

<Callout type="warn" title="Visit before the floor exit">
Do not descend with a full meter if you intend to use that Temple. The safehouse is not a substitute Temple stop.
</Callout>

## What can the different Temples help you do?

Treat the Temple as a specialised stop that offers interactions aligned with your Faith, then inspect its current choices before committing resources. Shopping, companions, forging, gambling, keys, and wisps lead to different kinds of opportunities. Prepare for the purpose of the visit instead of expecting every Temple to solve the same health or damage problem.

| Faith | Temple | Main purpose to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumerism | Shopping | Goods and purchase options |
| Agapeism | Cuteness | Egg and companion interactions |
| Artisanism | Forge | Weapon, Soul Shard, and badge tools |
| Fortunism | Gambling | Gambling machines and their risks |
| Thesaurism | Treasure | Locked rewards and a prize wheel |
| Umbrism | Shadow | Wisp management |
| Psychedelism | Acid | Its reward interaction |
| Dualism | Wisdom or Violence | The Temple corresponding to the active aspect |

<Accordion title="How should you prepare for a shopping or companion Temple?">
<p>For shopping, preserve coins for a purchase that solves a current problem. Entering the Temple does not make every optional interaction worth buying.</p>
<p>For a companion plan, check your eggs, hatching tools, and active squad before spending. Decide whether you need another companion, recovery support, or resources for an equipped Curio.</p>
</Accordion>

## When is changing Faith worth the cost?

Change Faith when the new option has a clear purpose and you can afford the switch. Floor one lets you make that decision without the later damage cost. On subsequent floors, weigh the hit against the benefit, check whether the current meter is full, and choose before entering a fight that depends on your Faith.

| Situation | Sensible action |
| --- | --- |
| First floor, still choosing a direction | Compare available Faiths before spending resources |
| Later floor, low health | Preserve survival unless the change is necessary for your plan |
| Current meter already full | Visit the Temple first; a full meter blocks switching |
| Your weapon or resources have changed | Reconsider whether the current Faith still matches useful actions |

<Accordion title="How can you separate a Faith decision from a damage gamble?">
<p>Make the purpose explicit: the new Faith should support a resource plan, a Temple visit, or a route condition. If you cannot name the benefit, continuing with the current option is a reasonable choice.</p>
<p>Check your health before interacting. A later-floor switch causes a hit; do not treat it as a harmless menu change.</p>
</Accordion>

Use the [boss preparation guide](/bosses/boss-guide/) before committing to an encounter. Temple rewards help most when you arrive with enough health and resources to use the resulting setup.
